## 美术提示

### 敌人

A full body chibi sticker of a pale ghost with a rounded white sheet body, two dark oval eyes, a small open mouth, and short stubby arms, drawn with a thick outline and no text. It floats full body alone on a flat magenta background.

### 首领

A full body chibi sticker of the Golden Horn King as a pale ghost with a rounded white sheet body, two dark oval eyes, a small open mouth, short stubby arms, and two curved golden horns, drawn with a thick outline and no text. He floats full body on a flat magenta background and is not dressed as Sun Wukong.

### 背景

A wide 16:9 children's storybook landscape for the generic theme and the title The Injured Monk. Paint a quiet mountain road under a pale blue sky with soft cream clouds, warm green slopes and brown earth below, pine trees and grey boulders only at the sides, and distant rounded peaks. A clear empty tan dirt path runs through the center, open and unoccupied. Soft watercolor colors. No characters, no people, no animals, no letters, and no writing.
