## 美术提示

### 敌人

A full body chibi sticker of a brown monkey with round ears, a peach face, a rounded torso, and a curled tail, drawn with a thick outline and no text. It stands alone on a flat magenta background.

### 首领

A full body chibi sticker of Patriarch Subodhi as a pale ghost with a rounded white sheet body, two dark oval eyes, a small open mouth, and short arms, drawn with a thick outline and no text. The old Taoist master floats on a flat magenta background and is not dressed as Sun Wukong.

### 背景

A wide 16:9 children's storybook landscape of the three-star cave, the hidden mountain temple of secret formulas: dark pine trees, pale stone steps, and a quiet cave shrine under a soft blue sky where three bright stars still glow. A clear empty dirt path runs through the center of the grassy ground toward the temple, with sky above and solid earth below, in gentle watercolor colors. No people, no animals, no characters, no letters, and no writing.
