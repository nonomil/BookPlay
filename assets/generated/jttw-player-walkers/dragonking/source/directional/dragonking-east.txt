Use case: transparent-ready 2D storybook game asset for a Journey to the West learning mini-game.
Character: original, child-safe East Sea Dragon King (东海龙王), an upright compact teal-blue dragon ruler with friendly eyes, short whiskers, small golden horns, sapphire robe with wave trim, coral sash, little pearl, and short clawed feet; never a long snake body.
Direction: side view facing RIGHT (east). Keep the same orthographic camera, scale, proportions, costume layers, palette, signature prop and foot baseline as the other directions. The north row must show the back of the same costume; east and west are true side views, not three-quarter views; south clearly shows the face.

Create exactly one sprite sheet on a perfectly flat #00ff00 chroma-key background, no scenery and no text. Exactly 8 equal portrait cells in a 4 columns x 2 rows grid, final sheet 1536x1024, each cell 384x512, with 12px green gutters. Read cells left-to-right on the top row, then left-to-right on the bottom row. One complete character per cell, centered, never clipped or overlapping, all feet on one shared absolute baseline near the bottom, generous transparent-ready margin, identical camera and lighting.

The first four cells are an in-place walk cycle with robe hem, whiskers, arms and tail tip moving while both feet stay on one baseline. The last four cells show a gentle water-magic cycle: lift the pearl, draw a small blue wave ribbon, let it arc around the body, then lower the pearl; no attack, no flooding and no other characters.
Frames 1-4 are walk poses in this order: contact, left-foot-forward, passing, right-foot-forward. Frames 5-8 are the action poses in this order: anticipation, action-open, action-peak, settle. The action prop must stay the same size and identity. Keep every frame readable at 36x48 pixels, with crisp dark-brown outline and softly textured children’s storybook fills.

Lighting and style: bright welcoming Chinese-fantasy palette, polished 2D game illustration, even light, no cast shadow. No green pixels on the character. no long serpentine tail, no trident, no aggressive expression, no extra characters, no water background. Also avoid motion blur, watermark, logo, typography, changing camera zoom, oval or stretched body, duplicate limbs, cropped hands or feet, matte lines, checkerboard or fake transparency.
