Use case: transparent-ready 2D storybook game asset for a Journey to the West learning mini-game.
Character: original, child-safe Heaven captain (天庭将领), a kind celestial captain with warm tan skin, neat black topknot, sky-blue cloud-trim robe under soft gold shoulder guards, a crimson sash, white cloud boots, and a small rolled report scroll; polished storybook official, not a warrior.
Direction: front view facing the viewer (south/down). Keep the same orthographic camera, scale, proportions, costume layers, palette, signature prop and foot baseline as the other directions. The north row must show the back of the same costume; east and west are true side views, not three-quarter views; south clearly shows the face.

Create exactly one sprite sheet on a perfectly flat #00ff00 chroma-key background, no scenery and no text. Exactly 8 equal portrait cells in a 4 columns x 2 rows grid, final sheet 1536x1024, each cell 384x512, with 12px green gutters. Read cells left-to-right on the top row, then left-to-right on the bottom row. One complete character per cell, centered, never clipped or overlapping, all feet on one shared absolute baseline near the bottom, generous transparent-ready margin, identical camera and lighting.

The first four cells are a dignified in-place walk cycle with a small shoulder bounce, robe hem movement and the report scroll held safely at the side. The last four cells show a messenger action: unfold the report scroll, raise one hand toward the Earth, point at a tiny light marker on the scroll, then salute and settle; no sword or combat.
Frames 1-4 are walk poses in this order: contact, left-foot-forward, passing, right-foot-forward. Frames 5-8 are the action poses in this order: anticipation, action-open, action-peak, settle. The action prop must stay the same size and identity. Keep every frame readable at 36x48 pixels, with crisp dark-brown outline and softly textured children’s storybook fills.

Lighting and style: bright welcoming Chinese-fantasy palette, polished 2D game illustration, even light, no cast shadow. No green pixels on the character. no spear, no sword, no helmet hiding the face, no stern or frightening expression, no extra celestial soldiers, no background palace. Also avoid motion blur, watermark, logo, typography, changing camera zoom, oval or stretched body, duplicate limbs, cropped hands or feet, matte lines, checkerboard or fake transparency.
